⬡ The Program

What the BSc Basic unlocks

A 4-year undergraduate degree leading to RN registration and the broadest set of career options in Bangladeshi nursing.

🎓

Who it's for

HSC pass students with biology background, targeting government and private nursing colleges across Bangladesh.

📚

What you gain

Full BSc in Nursing degree with 4 years of theory + clinical training, plus eligibility for MSN, NCLEX, and abroad.

🏥

Where it leads

Staff Nurse posts (BCS), private hospital roles, faculty career, MSN/MPH eligibility, abroad pathways.

⬡ Questions

Frequently asked

Everything you might want to know before enrolling.

Who is the BSc Basic program for? +

HSC pass students with science background (especially Biology) targeting BSc in Nursing admissions at BNMC, College of Nursing Mohakhali, and government nursing colleges nationwide.

Are the mocks in Bangla or English? +

Both. Every question is available in Bangla and English to match the actual admission paper format. You can switch language at any time during a mock.

How does the model test difficulty compare to the real exam? +

The Pulse model tests are calibrated to match — and often slightly exceed — past BNMC and College of Nursing papers. Students consistently report the real exam felt familiar.

Can I buy only the book without the mock series? +

Yes. The book and mock series are sold independently. The bundle is offered for students who want both at a 25% discount.

Do you offer in-person classes? +

Yes — Pulse for Nurses runs offline batches at our Shanir Akhra (Jatrabari) and Mohammadpur (Bashila) branches, with hostel facilities for outstation students. Online batches run alongside.
