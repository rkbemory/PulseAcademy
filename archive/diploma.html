⬡ The Program

What the Diploma unlocks

A 3-year diploma awarded by Bangladesh Nursing & Midwifery Council. Your entry pass into the nursing profession.

📖

Who it's for

HSC pass students (any group) targeting government nursing institutes nationwide. The most accessible nursing pathway.

💼

What you gain

3-year diploma, RN registration, and 2nd-class government job eligibility immediately upon completion.

🛤️

Where it leads

Government Staff Nurse posts, private hospital roles. With 2 years of work, eligibility for Post Basic BSc upgrade.

⬡ Questions

Frequently asked

Everything you might want to know before enrolling.

Who is the Diploma program for? +

HSC pass candidates targeting BNMC-affiliated nursing institutes nationwide. Open to both science and humanities stream HSC graduates with eligibility.

Are the mocks in Bangla or English? +

Both. Every question is available in Bangla and English to match the actual admission paper. You can switch language at any time.

After the Diploma, can I do BSc? +

Yes — after 2 years of registered work experience, you can apply for the Post Basic BSc program. We have a dedicated track for that prep.

Is the Pulse course Bangla-medium friendly? +

Yes. Notes, mocks, and rationales are all bilingual. Most of our offline batches are Bangla-medium with English terminology drilled in.

Do you offer in-person classes? +

Yes — Pulse for Nurses runs offline batches at our Shanir Akhra (Jatrabari) and Mohammadpur (Bashila) branches, with hostel facilities for outstation students. Online batches run alongside.
