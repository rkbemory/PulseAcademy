⬡ The Program

What the Midwifery diploma unlocks

A 3-year specialty diploma awarded by Bangladesh Nursing & Midwifery Council, focused on maternal and newborn care.

👶

Who it's for

HSC pass candidates (any group) drawn to maternal-newborn care, targeting BNMC-affiliated midwifery institutes nationwide.

🩺

What you gain

3-year specialty diploma, midwife registration, and direct entry into government and NGO midwifery roles.

🌐

Where it leads

Government Midwife posts (BCS), UN/NGO maternal health roles, abroad pathways for midwifery licensure (UK, Australia, Gulf).

⬡ Questions

Frequently asked

Everything you might want to know before enrolling.

Who is the Midwifery Diploma program for? +

HSC pass candidates (any group) targeting BNMC-affiliated midwifery institutes. The program is open to all genders, though most enrolled students are women.

Is the Midwifery Diploma the same as the Nursing Diploma? +

No — they are separate programs. Midwifery is a specialty diploma focused on maternal and newborn care; the Nursing Diploma is a general 3-year nursing program. Both are 3 years and BNMC-recognized.

Can I take both Diploma and Midwifery admission tests? +

Yes. Many candidates apply for both. The syllabus overlaps significantly, and our combined-prep batch covers both at a discounted bundle.

After Midwifery Diploma, can I work abroad? +

Yes. Midwifery licensure pathways exist for the UK (NMC), Australia (NMBA), and several Gulf countries. Our Study Abroad track maps each one.

Do you offer in-person classes? +

Yes — Pulse for Nurses runs offline batches at our Shanir Akhra (Jatrabari) and Mohammadpur (Bashila) branches, with hostel facilities for outstation students. Online batches run alongside.
