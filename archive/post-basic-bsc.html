⬡ The Program

What the Post Basic BSc unlocks

A 2-year bridge program for Diploma-qualified RNs to earn a full BSc in Nursing — and unlock MSN, government promotion, and abroad pathways.

👩‍⚕️

Who it's for

Diploma in Nursing graduates with at least 2 years of work experience, looking to upgrade to BSc.

📈

What you gain

BSc in Nursing degree in 2 years, equivalent to BSc Basic. Eligibility for MSN, MPH, NCLEX, and abroad licensure.

⬆️

Where it leads

Government 2nd-class promotion, faculty appointment, MSN admission, abroad credentialing pathways.

⬡ Questions

Frequently asked

Everything you might want to know before enrolling.

Who is the Post Basic BSc program for? +

Diploma in Nursing graduates (3-year diploma) with at least 2 years of registered work experience who want to upgrade to a full BSc degree.

Is PB-BSc equivalent to BSc Basic? +

Yes. After completing PB-BSc, you hold a BSc in Nursing degree equivalent to BSc Basic, with the same MSN/abroad eligibility.

How is this different from the MSN prep? +

PB-BSc focuses on nursing fundamentals across six core areas. MSN prep is more specialized and assumes BSc-level knowledge already in place.

Can I prepare while working full-time? +

Most of our PB-BSc students do exactly that. The mock series is designed to fit a 90-minute daily study block, with weekend full-length mocks.

Do you offer in-person classes? +

Yes — Pulse for Nurses runs offline batches at our Shanir Akhra (Jatrabari) and Mohammadpur (Bashila) branches, with hostel facilities for outstation students. Online batches run alongside.
