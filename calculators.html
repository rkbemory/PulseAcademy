⬡ FAQ

Nursing calculators — common questions

Are the nursing calculators free to use?

Yes. Every dosage, IV drip, BMI, GFR and other clinical calculator is free, runs instantly in your browser, and works offline.

What can the Pulse nursing calculators do?

They cover common bedside maths — drug dosage, IV flow and drip rates, BMI and BSA, creatinine clearance, paediatric fluids and more — each showing the working and a short interpretation.

Are these calculators safe to use for real patients?

They are for education and learning. Always verify results against your institution's protocol, the prescription, and your clinical judgement before acting.

Do I need to sign up or install anything to use them?

No. The calculators run instantly in any browser, free, with no sign-up, and work offline as a progressive web app.
