Nurse CV Builder — common questions

Is the Nurse CV Builder free?

Yes. You can build, preview and export a professional nurse CV to PDF for free, with no sign-up required.

What CV formats does it offer?

Several Europass-inspired templates with reorderable sections, a photo option, a CEFR language grid and an accent-colour picker.

Can I download my CV as a PDF?

Yes — the live A4 preview prints straight to PDF, and your CV is saved in your browser and to your account if you sign in.

Is the CV Builder suitable for international nursing jobs?

Yes. It is built in a clean, Europass-style format that recruiters and nursing employers abroad recognise.
