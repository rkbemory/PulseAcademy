⬡ Why Pulse

Built around how you actually learn.

  • Read a topic, then quiz it. Each topic discussion ends with a check-in quiz so the concept locks in before you move on.
  • Mock tests with rationales. Every model test gives a subject-wise breakdown — where you're strong, where you're weak, and which topic to revise.
  • Authored, not auto-generated. Content is grounded in our 4th-edition guides, public-health bodies (WHO, NIH), and ten years of past papers.
  • Free to practice. Everything is free — and you can now create a free account to save your progress and sync it across every device. Practice stays free, always.
