🛡️ Spot fake "government" circulars

  • Real government applications go through an official .gov.bd site or a Teletalk portal (like bpsc.teletalk.com.bd) — nothing else.
  • Application fees are paid only by SMS from a Teletalk number — never by bKash/Nagad transfer to a person.
  • No genuine recruiter ever asks for money to "confirm" a nursing job. If they do, walk away.
  • Verify your BNMC registration status yourself at bnmc.gov.bd — don't hand documents to middlemen.
