NCLEX-RN — common questions

Is Pulse for Nurses NCLEX-RN preparation free?

Yes. All 30 topic discussions, 20 question sets, and 5 NCLEX-style adaptive exams are completely free, with no sign-up needed to practise.

Can internationally-educated nurses use this for the NCLEX-RN?

Yes — it is built for internationally-educated nurses worldwide pursuing Registered Nurse licensure in the USA and Canada, and follows the same Client-Needs blueprint the real exam uses.

What is the NCLEX-RN exam?

It is the computer-adaptive licensing exam a nurse must pass to practise as a Registered Nurse in the United States (and used in Canada). It tests safe, effective clinical judgement rather than memorised facts.

Does it include adaptive (CAT) practice like the real test?

Yes. Five NCLEX-style adaptive exams adjust question difficulty to your performance and estimate your pass probability, mirroring the real computer-adaptive test.

How should I use this NCLEX page to study?

Read a topic in a weak area, take its 10-question check-in, build stamina with the 20-question sets, then sit an adaptive exam to gauge readiness — and repeat where you score lowest.
