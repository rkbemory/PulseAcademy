⬡ FAQ

Resource Hub — common questions

What is the Nursing Resource Hub?

A curated directory of free, trustworthy nursing resources — research databases, global health organisations, journals, and study-abroad application portals.

Is the Resource Hub free to use?

Yes. Every link is free to browse and grouped into clear categories for quick access.

What kinds of resources are included?

Research databases (such as Embase, Scopus and Web of Science), global health organisations, nursing journals, and portals for studying and working abroad.

Who is the Resource Hub for?

Nurses and nursing students who want vetted, free resources for study, research and international career planning in one place.
