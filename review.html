Smart Review — common questions

What is Smart Review?

A free spaced-repetition flashcard system that automatically resurfaces the questions you got wrong, scheduling them so they stick in long-term memory.

How does Smart Review work?

It captures the questions you miss in quizzes and reviews them on an SM-2-style schedule — sooner for hard cards, later for ones you know.

Is Smart Review free?

Yes. It works offline in your browser, free, and syncs to your account when you sign in.
