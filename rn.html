⬡ About

About the BNMC RN Exam

The Bangladesh Nursing & Midwifery Council (BNMC) administers the Registered Nurse licensing exam. Diploma in Nursing graduates must clear it to practice as an RN.

  • Format: MCQs across fundamental nursing, anatomy, physiology, nutrition, community health, child health, English, GK.
  • Difficulty: Foundational — 8th to 12th-grade biology plus diploma-level nursing concepts.
  • How to use this site: Read each topic once, take the check-in. When all topics feel solid, run through the 20 model tests in sequence.
  • Free, no login. Your scores are saved in your browser. Take any test as many times as you want.

⬡ FAQ

BNMC RN licensing — common questions

What is the BNMC RN licensing exam?

It is the Registered Nurse licensing examination of the Bangladesh Nursing and Midwifery Council (BNMC); Pulse for Nurses prepares you with topic discussions and model tests.

Is BNMC RN exam preparation free?

Yes — all 13 topic discussions with check-in MCQs, 20 model tests and 3 full simulation tests are completely free.

How many practice questions does the BNMC RN prep include?

Twenty model tests of 30 questions each plus three full 100-question simulations, each with a subject-wise evaluation of your performance.

How do I prepare for the BNMC RN licensing exam?

Read each topic and take its check-in, then practise the model tests and sit the full simulations to build exam stamina and confidence.
